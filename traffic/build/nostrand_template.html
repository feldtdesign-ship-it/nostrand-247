The corridorNostrand is a conveyor

One-way southbound, three lanes, a bus lane, and the B44 Select Bus every few minutes. This strip runs at the hour on the scrubber. Buses arrive at the real rate, drop and pick up the real number of riders, and the G spills its crowd from the corner of Lafayette. Cars are the recorder's count. Drag the hour and watch the block fill.

Nobody counts this sidewalk. The estimate adds what the transit data puts onto the block: bus riders at the five stops, G riders both ways, and a share of the neighbourhood walking to them, then halves it for the west side. It is an estimate assembled from measured pieces, not a measurement, and the share of G riders who walk this way is the soft part.

CamerasNone on this street

No public camera sees Nostrand Avenue here. The city's 973 traffic cameras thin out east of Flatbush. The nearest four are 1.3 to 2 km away and point at Atlantic Avenue and the expressway. They are on the live wall for completeness, and they are not evidence about this block.

Live wall of the same four: live-wall.html. A camera on the storefront itself is the honest fix.
